---
name: Care-plan completion and review policy
description: Why the reference PDF is a layout reference rather than a source of clinical defaults
---

Care-plan drafts are completed or revised in editable Word documents, not an
in-app editor. External Word edits do not sync back into the application.

**Why:** The user confirmed this workflow when adopting the Salveo layout.

**How to apply:** Do not add document editing, signature capture or Word re-import
as implicit parts of template work.

The reference's annual review example is not a policy change. Keep the quarterly
review default unless the user separately confirms a change. The reference's
sample goal/provider counts, synthetic answers, signatures and attachment names
are not clinical requirements or provider defaults.

**Why:** The supplied PDF is a completed synthetic test document; using its values
or annual dates as policy would misrepresent real clients.

**How to apply:** Treat future reference documents as layout/evidence-mapping
inputs, never as authority to fill unknowns or change review policy.
