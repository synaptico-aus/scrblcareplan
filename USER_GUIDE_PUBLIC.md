## Welcome to SCRBL 2.0

SCRBL 2.0 helps Australian aged care providers create compliant care plans quickly and efficiently. Our AI-powered platform transforms your assessment notes into structured care plans that meet all Australian regulatory requirements.

### What You Can Do

- **Generate Care Plans**: Transform assessment transcripts into Gold Standard care plans in minutes
- **Save and Manage**: Store all your care plans securely in one place
- **Export**: Download care plans as Word documents for editing and distribution
- **Manage Documents**: Upload and organize client-related documents
- **Team Collaboration**: Multiple team members can access and create care plans

### Compliance

All care plans generated comply with:
- Aged Care Quality Standards (1-8)
- Support at Home Program requirements
- Privacy Act 1988
- Australian data residency requirements

## Getting Started

### Creating Your Account

**First Time Registration:**

1. Click "Sign up" on the login page
2. Enter your organization name
3. Provide your details (first name, last name, email)
4. Create a password (minimum 6 characters)
5. Click "Create Account"

The first person to register for an organization becomes an administrator automatically.

**Logging In:**

1. Enter your email address
2. Enter your password
3. Click "Login"

You'll be directed to the Care Planner page where you can start creating care plans.

## Creating Care Plans

### Step-by-Step Process

**Step 1: Fill in Client Details**

- **Client Name**: Enter the client's full name (required)
- **Assessment Type**: Choose from Initial Assessment, Review Assessment, Reassessment, or Unplanned Review
- **Funding Type**: Select Home Care Package (HCP) or Support at Home (SAH)
- **Classification Level**: For Support at Home, select Level 1-8 based on the client's needs

**Step 2: Enter Your Assessment Notes**

In the "Assessment Transcript" field, include:

- **Client Information**: Age, address, language, living situation
- **Medical Conditions**: Current diagnoses and health issues
- **Medications**: All medications with dosages and frequencies
- **Allergies**: Any known allergies
- **Family and Support**: Family members and their involvement
- **Client Goals**: What the client wants to achieve (in their own words)
- **Risks**: Safety concerns, fall risks, cognitive issues
- **Services Needed**: Personal care, domestic help, allied health, etc.

**Tip**: The more detailed your notes, the better the care plan will be.

**Step 3: Generate the Care Plan**

1. Click "Generate Care Plan"
2. Wait 1-3 minutes for processing
3. Review the "Extracted Facts" panel to verify accuracy
4. Check the "Care Plan Preview" on the right

**Step 4: Save Your Work**

1. Scroll to the bottom of the preview panel
2. Enter a descriptive name for the plan (e.g., "Margaret Wilson - Initial Assessment Oct 2024")
3. Click "Save Care Plan"

## Understanding Your Care Plans

### Care Plan Sections

Your generated care plan includes 18 sections:

1. **Care Alerts**: Critical safety information
2. **My Care Plan**: Basic client demographics
3. **People Involved**: Who participated in developing the plan
4. **Cultural Values and Beliefs**: Cultural preferences and requirements
5. **Care Worker Preferences**: How the client prefers to receive services
6. **Preferred Support People**: Family members involved in care
7. **Informal Supports**: Friends, neighbors, community support
8. **Important Things to Know**: Personal information relevant to care
9. **Medical Information**: Conditions, medications, GP details
10. **Risk Assessment**: Identified risks and mitigation strategies
11. **Emergency Contacts**: Who to contact in emergencies
12. **My Goals**: SMARTA-formatted goals
13. **Budget and Services**: Service breakdown and costs
14. **Care Plan Consent**: Agreement and distribution information
15. **Prepared By**: Care partner and organization details
16. **Transcript Summary**: Key points mapped to Quality Standards
17. **Dignity of Risk**: Client's informed choices about risks

### SMARTA Goals

Each goal in your care plan follows the SMARTA framework:

- **S**pecific: Clear, detailed description of what the client wants
- **M**easurable: How progress will be tracked
- **A**chievable: Realistic based on client's abilities
- **R**elevant: Aligns with client's values and needs
- **T**ime-bound: Has a timeframe or review period
- **A**ction-oriented: Specific tasks and who does them

### Budget and Services Section

This section shows:
- Services the client needs based on their goals
- Hourly rates for each service
- Monthly cost breakdown
- Service categories (Clinical Care, Independence Support, Everyday Living)
- Total quarterly budget

**Note**: Service rates are configured by your organization administrator.

## Exporting Care Plans

### Export to Word

1. Open the care plan you want to export
2. Click "Export to Word" button
3. The file will download to your computer

**File Format**: The exported file is a fully editable Microsoft Word document (.doc) that you can:
- Edit and customize
- Add your organization's letterhead
- Share with clients and families
- Print for signatures
- Store in your client management system

### Printing

1. Click the "Print" button
2. Use your browser's print dialog
3. Print directly or save as PDF

## Managing Client Documents

### Uploading Documents

1. Open any saved care plan
2. Scroll to the "Client Documents" section
3. Click "Upload Document"
4. Select your file
5. Add a description (optional)
6. Click "Upload"

**Supported File Types**: PDF, JPG, PNG, DOC, DOCX  
**Maximum File Size**: 10MB per file

### Organizing Documents

- Documents are automatically organized by client name
- All documents for a client appear together regardless of which care plan you're viewing
- Documents are accessible to all users in your organization

### Viewing and Downloading

- Click any document to view it
- Click the download icon to save it to your computer
- Use the delete icon to remove documents (admin only)

## Admin Features

### User Management

**Adding New Users:**

1. Click your name in the top right
2. Select "User Management"
3. Click "Add New User"
4. Fill in their details
5. Assign a role (Admin or User)
6. Set monthly plan limit (optional)
7. Click "Create User"

**User Roles:**

- **Admin**: Can manage users, configure service pricing, and generate unlimited care plans
- **User**: Can generate, save, and export care plans

**Setting Plan Limits:**

You can limit how many care plans a user can generate per month:
- Leave blank for unlimited
- Enter a number (e.g., 50) to set a monthly limit
- Limits reset at the start of each calendar month

### Service Pricing Configuration

**Setting Up Service Rates:**

1. Go to User Management
2. Click "Service Pricing" tab
3. Add or edit services
4. Set hourly rates
5. Assign service categories

**Service Categories:**
- **Clinical Care**: Nursing, allied health, medication management
- **Independence Support**: Personal care, mobility assistance
- **Everyday Living**: Domestic assistance, meals, shopping, transport

**Important**: Service rates appear in the Budget section of care plans. Configure these before generating plans for accurate cost estimates.

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Solutions

**Care plan generation is slow (taking more than 3 minutes)**
- Long, detailed transcripts take longer to process
- Wait patiently - the system is working
- If it takes longer than 5 minutes, try refreshing the page and generating again

**I can't find a saved care plan**
- Clear the search box to see all plans
- Check you're logged into the correct organization
- Refresh the page
- Care plans are organization-specific - you can only see plans created by your organization

**Export to Word button doesn't work**
- Allow popups from this website in your browser settings
- Try a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, or Edge recommended)
- Check your download folder - the file may have downloaded automatically

**Service pricing shows "$X/hour" instead of actual rates**
- Your administrator needs to configure service rates
- Go to User Management → Service Pricing
- Once rates are set, regenerate the care plan to see updated pricing

**Document upload fails**
- Check file size is under 10MB
- Ensure file type is PDF, JPG, PNG, DOC, or DOCX
- Try a different file format
- Check your internet connection

**I've reached my plan generation limit**
- Contact your organization administrator
- They can increase your monthly limit
- Limits reset at the start of each month

**I forgot my password**
- Contact your organization administrator to reset it
- They can update your password in User Management

## Privacy and Compliance

### Data Security

- All data is stored securely in Australian servers (Sydney region)
- Your organization's data is completely isolated from other organizations
- No data is shared between organizations
- All passwords are encrypted
- Secure, encrypted connections for all data transfers

### Privacy Act 1988

SCRBL 2.0 complies with the Privacy Act 1988:
- Data stored in Australia
- Role-based access control
- Secure authentication
- No cross-organization data access
- Industry-standard security measures

### Aged Care Quality Standards

All care plans are structured to demonstrate compliance with all 8 Aged Care Quality Standards:
1. Consumer dignity and choice
2. Ongoing assessment and planning with consumers
3. Personal care and clinical care
4. Services and supports for daily living
5. Organisation's service environment
6. Feedback and complaints
7. Human resources
8. Organisational governance

### Support at Home Program

Care plans generated for Support at Home funding include:
- SMARTA goals framework
- Classification levels (1-8)
- Service categorization (Clinical Care, Independence Support, Everyday Living)
- Quarterly budget allocation
- Compliance with program requirements effective July 2025

- Include comprehensive assessment notes

- Include family involvement details
- Specify preferred service times and days

**Care Plan Quality:**

- Update care plans when client circumstances change
